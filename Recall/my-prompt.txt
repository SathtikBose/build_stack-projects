### UI Design Prompt: Futuristic Glassmorphic Dark Mode

#### Theme & Aesthetic
* **Vibe:** Futuristic, sophisticated, data-driven, and sleek. It uses high-contrast glowing elements against an ultra-dark background to create depth and a premium, high-tech feel.
* **Style:** Modern **Glassmorphism** mixed with **Neon Glow / Synthwave** undertones. Elements should have soft background blurs, subtle translucent borders, and glowing vector accents.

---

#### Color Palette
* **Background:** Deep, dark midnight-purple gradient (e.g., transitioning from a near-black `#0F0022` to a rich, dark plum `#1A0035`). 
* **Component Cards:** Translucent, glassmorphic dark purple with a heavy background blur (15px–20px) and a subtle, soft internal gradient (e.g., `#220044` at 40% opacity).
* **Borders:** Ultra-thin, crisp borders for cards using a light purple or white with low opacity (e.g., `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`) to define edges without looking heavy.
* **Primary Text & Main Icons:** Crisp, high-contrast pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
* **Secondary Text & Muted Icons:** Soft, faded lavender-grey (`#A39BB0` to `#CCCCCC`) for secondary hierarchy.
* **Accents & Glows:** Vibrant, luminous electric purples (`#9B51E0`) for active states, paired with soft neon blue/cyan (`#82CAFF`) for glowing data lines, data points, and active indicators.

---

#### Typography
* **Font Family:** Clean, highly legible geometric Sans-Serif (e.g., SF Pro Display, Inter, or Roboto).
* **Weights:** Primarily **Bold** for key data/numbers, **Medium** for section headers, and **Regular** for body text and descriptions.
* **Hierarchy:**
  * *Hero Numbers/Metrics:* Large, heavy, and striking (approx. 28pt–32pt, Bold) to make data like "24" or "67" immediately pop.
  * *Section Headers:* Clean and prominent (approx. 18pt, Medium) with generous spacing.
  * *Card Titles & Labels:* Compact and clear (approx. 12pt–14pt, Medium).
  * *Micro-copy & Statuses:* Small, uppercase, or slightly muted text (approx. 10pt, Bold for dates/days; Regular for timestamps).

---

#### Key Visual Elements & Textures
* **Gradients & Lighting:** Soft, ambient backlighting (radial blurs) behind text headers to create a "halo" or "wave of light" effect.
* **UI Data Shading:** Line graphs and bar charts should use smooth vector curves with a vertical gradient fill that fades out towards the bottom, topped with bright, glowing data point markers.
* **Pill-Shaped Components:** Buttons, active tabs, and version tags use heavily rounded, pill-shaped geometry rather than sharp corners.
