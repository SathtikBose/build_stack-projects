# Product Vision

NewsFlow is a premium-looking modern news application designed with Glassmorphism and Liquid Glass UI principles.

The goal is to create an app that feels like a premium social media application rather than a typical news reader.

Users should experience:

* Smooth animations
* Immersive scrolling
* Fast news consumption
* Beautiful article reading experience
* Instagram/Reels inspired Feed
* Apple-inspired Glass UI
* Material 3 Dynamic Colors
