Food Ingredient and Nutrition Tracking Scanner
Context:
Consumers often find it difficult to quickly understand the
ingredients and nutritional value of packaged food products while
shopping. Although food labels provide this information, they are
often complex and not easy to interpret at a glance
Challenge:
There is a need for a simple solution that allows users to easily
identify food products and access clear information about
ingredients, calories, and nutritional values, along with guidance
that helps them make informed dietary choices.
Goal:
Develop a mobile application that allows users to scan food items
using an image, QR code, or barcode scanner to retrieve ingredient
lists, calories, and nutritional information from reliable databases.
The application should also present helpful insights such as whether
the product is generally safe to consume, should be eaten in
moderation, or requires consideration for specific diets, allergies,
or health conditions, along with other relevant nutritional details
that assist users in making healthier food decisions.
